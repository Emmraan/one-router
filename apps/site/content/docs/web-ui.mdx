## Chats and the composer

Start a new chat from the sidebar, use the header to choose a model, and type your message in the composer. Attach files to a message with the attachment button or by dropping them into the workspace. While a reply streams, you can stop generation.

## Saved and temporary chats

Find past chats with sidebar search and pin chats you want to keep near the top. Use the temporary chat toggle for a conversation that stays in memory and out of history unless you choose to save it. Close the tab or reload and unsaved temporary chats are lost.

## Workspace settings

Open **Settings** to change the light, dark, or system appearance; change your password; or open **Import / export**. Use **Providers** to change provider credentials and models. [Try the sample workspace](/#workspace) without an account or provider call.
