## Is there a hosted account?

ONE ROUTER is self-hosted. The [sample workspace](/#workspace) is an interactive preview with sample replies. It does not require an account or send requests to an AI provider.

## Can multiple people sign in?

The workspace has one owner account. Teams, shared seats, and separate user workspaces are not part of this version.

## Does installing it include model access?

No. Bring credentials for the hosted providers you want to use, or run a local Ollama model. Your providers bill you under their own terms.

## Can I switch hosts later?

Yes. Move your workspace using an encrypted archive, or restore the database, matching key, and attachments on another deployment with compatible storage. Read [backup and restore](/docs/backup/) before you migrate.
