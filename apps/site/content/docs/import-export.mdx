---
title: Import and export
description: Move or back up a workspace with an encrypted portable archive.
---

## Export your workspace

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Import / export**.
2. Enter a new export passphrase. Store it separately from the downloaded `.onerouter` file.
3. Download the archive and keep it in protected storage.

Provider credentials are inside the encrypted archive. Treat the archive and passphrase like other backups of sensitive data.

## Import into another installation

Sign in to the destination instance. In **Import / export**, choose the archive and enter its passphrase. Review the preview and choose whether to merge or replace. Replacement removes existing workspace data in scope; export the destination first if you might need it back.

The destination keeps its owner account and encryption key. Deployment settings, owner passwords, active sessions, and the gateway token are managed separately.
